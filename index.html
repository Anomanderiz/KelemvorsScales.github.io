Boss Attacks

Combo Group: give rows the same group name when the multiattack lets the boss use them "in any combination" (e.g. Claw and Rend both 2 uses, group flex). The group makes as many attacks as its largest Uses/round, each row is used at most its own Uses/round, and every attack goes to the option with the best expected damage, so damage is not double-counted.

Encounter Balancer

Two goals, checked by simulating thousands of fights: by the end of round 6 a PC is down or at 25% HP or less without healing, and the boss dies no earlier than round 6 and no later than round 11. Tune Everything sets boss damage and HP to hit both, and adds Legendary Resistance pacing only if HP alone can't.
